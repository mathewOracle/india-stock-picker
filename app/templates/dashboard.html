How suggestions are made

Every stock runs through three independent analyses. Each check scores from -1 to +1, weighted, and each pillar maps to 0-100 (50 = neutral).

  • Fundamental ({{ (weights.fundamental * 100) | int }}%): ROE, 3-year revenue & EPS CAGR, margin trend, debt/equity, interest cover, cash conversion, free cash flow, Piotroski F-score, P/E vs sector peers, PEG, forward P/E, analyst consensus.
  • Technical ({{ (weights.technical * 100) | int }}%): 200-DMA trend, moving-average stack, golden/death cross, ADX, Supertrend, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, Bollinger %B, on-balance volume, volume surges, relative strength vs Nifty.
  • Chart ({{ (weights.chart * 100) | int }}%): swing structure (higher highs/lows), 60-day regression channel, 52-week breakouts, support/resistance, Bollinger squeeze, pullback setups, double tops/bottoms, candlestick patterns, RSI divergence.
