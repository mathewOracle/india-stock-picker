Intraday indicators: what each one tells you

Technical (today's tape, 70% of bias)

VWAP & bands
Volume-weighted average price: the institutional benchmark. Above = buyers in control. The 2-sigma bands mark stretched extremes.
Opening range (09:15-09:30)
The first 15 minutes set the day's battle lines. A break signals who won the open.
CPR & floor pivots
Levels from yesterday's high/low/close. A narrow CPR hints at a trending day, a wide one at a range day. R1/R2/S1/S2 are classic targets.
Previous-day high/low
Breaking yesterday's extremes expands the range and triggers stops.
EMA 9/21, RSI(14), Supertrend on 5-minute bars
Short-term momentum, overbought/oversold, and a trailing-stop trend filter.
Relative volume (RVOL)
Today's volume against the average of prior days at the same time of day. Above 1.5x means real participation.
Gap & strength vs Nifty
Whether the opening gap holds or fades, and whether the stock leads or lags the index today.

Context: the "fundamental" layer (30% of bias)

Fundamentals barely move a stock within one day. For day trading they act as filters and a tilt, not a reason to trade.

Liquidity (avg daily traded value)
Below Rs {{ min_value }} Cr/day, spreads and slippage eat the edge. Hard filter.
Daily volatility (ATR%)
You need range to make money: under {{ (min_atr * 100) | round | int }}% is a hard filter, and above 4% means size down.
Results / event risk
On results day, gaps and whipsaws dominate. Hard filter on the day itself, caution within 3 days.
Daily-chart trend
Trade with the higher timeframe: longs in daily uptrends, shorts in downtrends.
Business quality
A small tilt: dips in quality names tend to get bought, rallies in weak ones sold.
Sector flow & beta
Sector peers moving together add conviction. High beta means bigger swings, so use smaller size.

Day-trading risk rules (non-negotiable)

  • Risk at most 1% of capital per trade. The Qty column sizes the position for you.
  • Set a daily loss limit (for example 3R) and stop trading for the day when it is hit.
  • Place the stop with the order. Never move it further away, and never average down a loser.
  • No new entries after 14:45. Close everything before the broker's auto square-off (about 15:15-15:20).
  • Yahoo bars can lag by a few minutes. Confirm prices in your broker terminal before acting.
